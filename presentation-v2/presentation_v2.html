LITERATURE REVIEW

Evolution of AMC

From statistical methods to deep learning.

  • 1.0
    Traditional

    Likelihood tests, Cumulants. Brittle below 0dB.

  • 2.0
    Machine Learning

    SVM, KNN. Feature engineering required.

  • 3.0
    Deep Learning

    CNNs, LSTMs. Learn directly from I/Q data.

KEY STUDIES

Abd-Elaziz (2023)

Robust CNN with asymmetric kernels. 96.5% accuracy at 0dB.

Zhang et al. (2023)

MoE-AMC: Mixture of Experts. 71.8% avg accuracy across wide SNR.

Zhang et al. (2023) - DAE

Dual-residual DAE. 75% gains in low SNR (-12dB to 8dB).

Jagannath (2022)

OTA Validation. >98% accuracy on USRP testbed.

RESEARCH GAPS

Low SNR Collapse

Most models fail below -5dB.

Simulation Only

Lack of OTA validation in many studies.

OUR APPROACH

Integrate Denoising Autoencoder (DAE) with AMC.

DATA COLLECTION & PREP

Datasets

  • RF Signal Data: Real SDR captures.
  • RadioML 2018.01A: Synthetic benchmark.
  • MIGOU-MOD: IoT OTA traces.

Preprocessing

Segmentation (1024 samples), Normalization (Zero-mean), Augmentation (AWGN, Fading).

DAE ARCHITECTURE

Type: Conv1D Encoder-Decoder

Blocks: 3 Downsampling, 3 Upsampling

Bottleneck: 128 dimensions

Loss: MSE + L1 Penalty

AMC CLASSIFIER

Type: 1D CNN

Layers: 4 Convolutional Blocks

Features: Squeeze-and-Excitation (Channel Attention)

Output: Softmax (Modulation Class)
