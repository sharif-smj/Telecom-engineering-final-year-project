Neural Network Architecture

The DAE-AMC system consists of a Denoising Autoencoder followed by a 1D CNN Classifier

🔗 Network Architecture Diagram
📥
Encoder (DAE)
Compresses the noisy signal into a lower-dimensional latent representation, learning to extract essential features while filtering noise.
Conv1D(64) Conv1D(32) Conv1D(16)
💎
Bottleneck
The compressed representation that captures the signal's core structure. Noise cannot be reconstructed from this compressed form.
Latent Dim: 8 Dense(64)
📤
Decoder (DAE)
Reconstructs the clean signal from the bottleneck representation, effectively removing the noise component.
Conv1DTranspose(16) Conv1DTranspose(32) Conv1DTranspose(64)
🎯
AMC Classifier
1D CNN that takes the cleaned signal and classifies it into one of several modulation types (BPSK, QPSK, 8PSK, 16QAM, 64QAM).
Conv1D(128) GlobalPool Dense(5) Softmax
