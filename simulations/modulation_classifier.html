📖 Understanding Modulation Classification

What is Modulation?
Modulation encodes digital data onto radio waves by varying amplitude, phase, or frequency. Higher-order schemes like 64QAM pack more bits per symbol but require cleaner signals.
Why SNR Matters
Signal-to-Noise Ratio determines how clearly symbols can be distinguished. At low SNR, constellation points blur together, making classification challenging without DAE enhancement.
AMC Classification
Automatic Modulation Classification uses machine learning to identify the modulation scheme from raw I/Q samples. Confidence scores indicate how certain the classifier is.
DAE Enhancement
The Denoising Autoencoder pre-processes noisy signals, recovering clean constellation patterns. This allows accurate classification even at SNR levels as low as -10 dB.
