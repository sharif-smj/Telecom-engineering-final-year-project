# Decision-Maker Map

Status date: April 22, 2026

Official 2026 reviewers are not publicly listed. This file maps the strongest public proxies tied to NCC quality control, hosting, sector direction, and the 2025 moderation environment.

## How to read this file

- `Proxy strength` is about how directly this person likely reflects the conference's taste or strategic direction.
- `Lens` is the likely question they would ask while hearing or reading a paper.
- `Alignment cue` is what our paper should make easy for that proxy to endorse.

## Practical reading of the map

If we satisfy the likely concerns of the public proxies above, our paper will feel aligned even if the private reviewer list differs.

The overlap across the proxies is strong:

- local problem first
- rigorous but readable engineering
- deployability and scale
- usefulness to operators, regulators, or underserved communities
- clear economic or service value
- inclusive national impact

## Resulting paper posture

Our best posture is:

- infrastructure problem
- AI-enabled solution
- service and market value
- regulator and operator relevance
- realistic path to deployment

Our weakest posture is:

- model-first jargon
- vague deployment story
- no affordability or scale logic
- claims that outrun the available evidence
