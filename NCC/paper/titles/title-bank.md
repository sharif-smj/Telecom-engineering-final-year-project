# Title Bank

Status date: April 23, 2026

Purpose:

- Keep strong title options in one place before we commit to a final direction.
- Favor outcome-first, reviewer-friendly phrasing.

Selection rules:

- Lead with the network or service outcome
- Keep the method visible but secondary
- Include Uganda when possible
- Avoid acronym-heavy openings
- Avoid `on-device` unless the device-performance claims are verified
- Avoid `without new towers` unless the wording is clearly motivational rather than empirical

## Strongest current candidates

1. Extending Effective GSM Coverage in Rural Uganda Through Signal Denoising
2. Signal Denoising for Improved Edge-of-Coverage GSM Service in Rural Uganda
3. Improving Effective GSM Service Reach in Rural Uganda Using Denoising Autoencoders
4. Denoising Weak GSM Signals to Support Rural Coverage Extension in Uganda
5. Recovering Usable Cell-Edge GSM Signals for Rural Uganda Coverage Expansion

## Slightly more technical variants

1. Improving Low-SNR GSM Signal Recovery for Rural Uganda Through Denoising Autoencoders
2. A Denoising-First Pipeline for Edge-of-Coverage GSM Signal Recovery in Uganda
3. Enhancing Cell-Edge GSM Signal Interpretation in Rural Uganda Using Denoising and Classification

## Variants that should stay as backups only

Reason:

- They are accurate, but more algorithm-first than winner-style.

1. Low-SNR GSM Modulation Recognition in Rural Uganda Using a Denoising Autoencoder Pipeline
2. DAE-Assisted GSM Signal Classification for Weak and Noisy Rural Coverage Conditions
3. Robust GSM Signal Denoising and Classification for Cell-Edge Environments in Uganda

## Variants to avoid until verification exists

Reason:

- These are attractive but currently outrun the evidence rules in the claim ledger.

1. On-Device GSM Signal Denoising for Rural Uganda
2. Extending 2G Coverage Without New Towers in Rural Uganda
3. On-Device GSM Signal Denoising for Rural Uganda: Extending 2G Coverage Without New Towers

## Quick evaluation checklist

- Does the title sound useful outside the lab?
- Would a UCC or UCUSAF reviewer understand the value immediately?
- Is the wording honest about what we actually tested?
- Does it sound like a paper that fits Track 1 before Track 4?

## Preferred current direction

- Best balance of ambition and honesty: `Extending Effective GSM Coverage in Rural Uganda Through Signal Denoising`
- Safest technically precise option: `Signal Denoising for Improved Edge-of-Coverage GSM Service in Rural Uganda`
