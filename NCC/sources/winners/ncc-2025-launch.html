  • NCC Launch (1st August)

    • Launch of the 9th National Conference on Communications

    August 1, 2025 - The Commission has officially launched the 9th edition of the National Conference on Communications (NCC 2025) at its headquarters in Bugolobi. This annual flagship conference, proudly sponsored by UCC, provides a strategic platform for academia, industry, government, and students to engage in meaningful dialogue on critical issues shaping Uganda's digital ecosystem.


    Scheduled for October 2025, this year's event is being organized in partnership with ISBAT University and Gulu University. NCC continues to play a pivotal role in advancing research, innovation, and sustainable ICT development in Uganda, aligning with UCC's broader mandate of facilitating digital transformation across the country.


    Speaking at the launch, UCC Executive Director emphasized the importance of research and innovation in addressing complex societal challenges. "As scientists, researchers, academics, and innovators, we must remain focused on generating solutions to the complex problems facing our society, Whether through disruptive technologies or progressive policies, research remains the backbone of successful digital transformation," he noted.


    He reaffirmed UCC's commitment to acting as a facilitator and enabler of ICT development, in pursuit of a digitally inclusive economy that benefits all Ugandans.

    Meet our NCC Advisory Board Members

    Dedicated professionals driving our success

    Dr. Edwin Mugume

    Lecturer, Department of Communication & Computer Engineering, Makerere University

    He was the Technical Program Chair of the 5th NCC (2019), and the General Chair of the 6th NCC (2020) and 7th NCC (2022). He is currently the Specialist Editor for the SAIEE Africa Research Journal, leading the drive to enhance publications from the East African region. He is a regular reviewer for leading IEEE journals and conferences and has been a technical committee member of several international IEEE conferences. He is a Member of IEEE.

     

    Mr. James Beronda

    Director UCUSAF (Uganda Communications Commission)

    A senior ICT professional and project manager with extensive experience managing ICT for Development (ICT4D) projects. Most of this experience has been in senior management roles, providing hands-on leadership and technical oversight in the design, execution, scheduling, and monitoring of these projects. Currently serves as the Director of the Uganda Communications Universal Service and Access Fund (UCUSAF) at the Uganda Communications Commission.

    Dr. Roseline Nyongarwizi Akol

    Lecturer, Department of Electrical & Computer Engineering Makerere University

    She contributed to the initial conceptualization of the NCC and has been in the business of human capital development of Electrical, Computer & Telecommunications Engineers in Uganda for many years. Her current research interest is in wireless technologies, Internet of Things (IoT), smart grids and smart technologies. Dr. Akol is passionate about harnessing and growing resources.

    Mr. Denis Kahindi

    Director Home Broadband Business (Airtel Uganda)

    He is a Director at Airtel Uganda and the former Chief Executive Officer at Liquid Intelligent Technologies – Uganda.

    He possesses proven senior management and operational leadership skills from over 15 years of experience in the Telecommunications and ICT industry.

    Dr. Godfrey Kibalya Mirondo

    Lecturer, Department of Computer Engineering & Informatics, Busitema University

    Dr. Mirondo serves as a reviewer, guest editor, and technical program committee member for several International Journals and conferences. His research interests include Applied Artificial Intelligence, Agentic AI, AI Ethics, Edge Computing, Network Slicing and Network Automation, with over 35 publications in leading international journals and conferences.

    He is currently a postdoctoral fellow with the CECAP11 Early Career Research program. He previously served as Senior Research and Innovation Engineer (AI) at Nearby Computing S.L., Barcelona, Spain and has participated in numerous EU funded projects.

    Ms. Barbara Birungi

    Director Hive Colab

    Barbara Birungi is a Ugandan social entrepreneur, technologist, mentor, and educator. She serves as the Director of Hive Colab, an innovation hub, business accelerator, and incubator for East African technology startups, with a mission to build local tech capacity across the region. Barbara is also the founder of Women in Technology Uganda (WITU), an organization dedicated to empowering women in the tech industry through networking, training, mentoring, and partnerships. WITU aims to increase the number of women entrepreneurs in tech, encourage girls to pursue STEM education, and close the gender gap in Uganda's technology sector.

    About us

    The National Conference on Communications (NCC) is a multiday event that is organised by the Uganda Communications Commission (UCC) to convene researchers, industry, government and students to showcase ICT research, debate policy, and accelerate home-grown digital innovation in the sector. It has been organised since 2011, hosted by Institutions including Makerere University, Mbarara University of Science and Technology, Ndejje University, Kyambogo University, ISBAT and Gulu Universities.
