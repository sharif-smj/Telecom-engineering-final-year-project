About us

The National Conference on Communications (NCC) is a multiday event that is organised by the Uganda Communications Commission (UCC) to convene researchers, industry, government and students to showcase ICT research, debate policy, and accelerate home-grown digital innovation in the sector. It has been organised since 2011, hosted by Institutions including Makerere University, Mbarara University of Science and Technology, Ndejje University, Kyambogo University, ISBAT and Gulu Universities.
