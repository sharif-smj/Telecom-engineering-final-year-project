NCC 2026 Paper Publishing Masterclasses 
NCC Conference 2025

VIDEO LINK

Publishing Masterclasses will be held to equip authors with essential research and writing skills. The series will focus on conducting rigorous literature reviews, mastering research methodologies, and effectively using tools for data analysis, referencing, typesetting, and citation. Participants will also gain hands-on experience with LaTeX for professional paper formatting and 10 manuscript preparation.

A total of six sessions will be held, with four of them held in-person at different universities, and two sessions held online to ensure wider reach. Universities in Kampala will be invited to attend the first masterclass held at Makerere University. These masterclasses will enhance prospective authors’ confidence and ability to produce and submit high-quality, publishable work to the conference.

About us

The National Conference on Communications (NCC) is a multiday event that is organised by the Uganda Communications Commission (UCC) to convene researchers, industry, government and students to showcase ICT research, debate policy, and accelerate home-grown digital innovation in the sector. It has been organised since 2011, hosted by Institutions including Makerere University, Mbarara University of Science and Technology, Ndejje University, Kyambogo University, ISBAT and Gulu Universities.
